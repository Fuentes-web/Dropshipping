The Manifest Guarantee

  • Delivered in 5 days or your shipping is refunded.
  • Duties and taxes prepaid — the price you see is the price you pay.
  • 30-day returns, prepaid label included, no restocking fee.
  • Broken in transit? Replaced before you send anything back.
How fast is delivery, really?

2–5 business days to most of North America, the UK, the EU, Australia and East Asia. 5–8 days elsewhere. Every parcel leaves the warehouse within 24 hours of the order, and the countdown you see on the product page is the real one.

Will I pay customs or import duties?

No. We ship DDP — delivered duty paid — to all 180 countries we serve. The amount at checkout is the total. There is no second invoice and no customs letter.

What if it doesn't fit, or I change my mind?

Send it back within 30 days for a full refund. The return label is prepaid and printed from your order page. Refunds are issued the day the parcel is scanned by the carrier.

Where are the products made?

It depends on the category — audio in Shenzhen and Vietnam, ceramics in Portugal, footwear in Ho Chi Minh City, luggage in Ubrique. Every product page lists the factory region, the materials and the audit status of the facility.

How do I track my order?

You get a waybill number the moment the parcel is scanned out, plus push updates at every handover. Or paste the number into the tracker above at any time.
